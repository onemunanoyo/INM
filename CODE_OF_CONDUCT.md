# Code of Conduct

INM is a technical and cultural benchmark project. Because the subject matter includes adult media, long-running internet memes, coarse expressions, and references to real-world performers, contributors are expected to keep discussions precise, respectful, and focused on the benchmark.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss people primarily as work/meme-level entities when that is sufficient for the benchmark.
- Cite sources and distinguish verified facts from community lore or uncertain claims.
- Correct mistakes without attacking the person who made them.
- Keep review comments specific to the item, source, schema, or implementation.
- Respect disagreement about ambiguous transcription, meme provenance, or classification.
- Use content warnings or neutral wording when a technical discussion can avoid unnecessary explicit detail.

## Unacceptable behavior

The following are not acceptable in project spaces:

- harassment, threats, personal attacks, or targeted slurs;
- attempting to identify, doxx, expose, or speculate about private individuals;
- publishing personal information that is not necessary for benchmark construction;
- directing meme phrases at another contributor in a hostile or degrading way;
- sexual harassment or unwanted sexualized comments;
- deliberately introducing fabricated citations or knowingly false benchmark evidence;
- repeated disruption after maintainers have asked that a discussion return to the technical issue.

## Scope

These expectations apply to issues, pull requests, discussions, review comments, repository documentation, and other project-managed spaces.

They also apply when someone is representing the project publicly, for example by speaking on behalf of INM or publishing official benchmark results.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions that violate these guidelines and may restrict participation when necessary to protect contributors or the integrity of the project.

When possible, moderation should be proportional and documented. Good-faith factual mistakes, transcription disagreements, and ordinary review disputes should be handled as technical issues rather than misconduct.

## 日本語要約

INMでは、題材そのものの俗語性と、プロジェクト上の対人態度を分けて考えます。

問題文や資料上で必要な語録を扱うことと、他の参加者に対して攻撃的な表現を向けることは別です。レビューでは人ではなく、問題・根拠・実装を批評してください。

また、出演者等の実世界での個人特定、私人の詮索、個人情報の収集・暴露は本プロジェクトの目的外です。

不確かな情報は不確かであると明示し、訂正は歓迎する形で行ってください。
